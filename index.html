About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence at Renmin University of China, advised by Prof. Xu Chen. I am a member of the AI-Engine Lab. I joined the integrated master's–Ph.D. program in 2022 and expect to graduate in 2028. Previously, I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Wuhan University of Technology in 2022.

My research focuses on LLM-powered recommender systems and personalized agents. I study reasoning for recommendation, user intent understanding, and interactive agents with long-term memory. I am also interested in faithful user simulation and evaluation, connecting algorithmic advances with real-world applications.

I have worked on industrial recommendation at Taobao through the RecGPT series (V1 · V2 · V3), and on reasoning for industrial ranking through OnePiece. My recent research interests include efficient long-sequence modeling for large language models, personalized memory, and personalized agents.

Outside of research, I enjoy 🏀 basketball, 🏃 running and other sports, as well as 🎬 movies and 🎵 music. 💭 A long-term dream of mine is to build a one-person company someday.

✨ Selected Publications

  • RecGPT-V3

    arXiv 2026🏆 Best Paper🚀 Deployed

    RecGPT-V3 Technical Report

    A stateful, hybrid-modal LLM recommender that reasons over text and Semantic IDs, with a Memory Hub for long-term user memory and latent intent reasoning. In Taobao's homepage feed it lifts GMV by 3.97% while cutting serving cost by 52.4%.

    First authorPaper

  • RecGPT-V2

    arXiv 2025🚀 Deployed

    RecGPT-V2 Technical Report

    Restructures intent reasoning as a hierarchical multi-agent system, with meta-prompted explanations, constrained RL and an Agent-as-a-Judge evaluator. Cuts GPU use by 60% and lifts CTR by 2.98% in Taobao A/B tests.

    First authorPaper

  • RecGPT-V1

    TOIS 2026🚀 Deployed

    RecGPT: A User Intent-Centric Next-Generation LLM-Powered Recommender System in Industrial Practice

    Puts user intent at the center of the recommendation pipeline, using LLMs for interest mining, item retrieval and explanation generation. Fully deployed on the Taobao app.

    First authorPaper

  • OnePiece

    KDD 2026🌟 Oral🚀 Deployed

    OnePiece: Bringing Context Engineering and Reasoning to Industrial Cascade Ranking System

    Brings LLM-style context engineering and block-wise latent reasoning into industrial retrieval and ranking models. Deployed in Shopee's main search, with over 2% GMV/UU gain.

    Co-first authorPaper

  • ReaRec

    TKDE 2026

    Think Before Recommend: Unleashing the Latent Reasoning Power for Sequential Recommendation

    The first inference-time computing framework for recommendation. Implicit multi-step reasoning raises the performance ceiling of sequential recommenders by roughly 30% to 50%.

    First authorPaperCode

  • RecBot

    KDD 2026🚀 Deployed

    Interactive recommendation agent with active user commands

    Lets users steer the recommendation feed with natural-language commands, through a Parser agent and a Planner agent. Improves both user satisfaction and business metrics in long-term online tests.

    First authorPaper

  • RecVerse

    arXiv 2026

    Towards Faithful Simulation of Human Shopping Behavior

    A GUI-grounded agent that simulates real shopping sessions from screenshots, using hierarchical memory and trajectory-level RL. Also releases the USB benchmark.

    First authorPaper

  • LoopCTR

    arXiv 2026

    LoopCTR: Unlocking the Loop Scaling Power for Click-Through Rate Prediction

    Scales CTR models by looping shared layers instead of adding parameters. Trained with multiple loops, it beats all baselines even with zero loops at inference.

    First authorPaper
