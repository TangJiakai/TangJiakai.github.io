About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Gaoling School of Artificial Intelligence at Renmin University of China, advised by Prof. Xu Chen. I joined the integrated master's–Ph.D. program in 2022 and expect to graduate in 2028. Previously, I received my bachelor's degree in Computer Science and Technology from Wuhan University of Technology in 2022.

My research focuses on LLM-powered recommender systems and intelligent agents. I study reasoning for recommendation, user intent understanding, and interactive agents with long-term memory. I am also interested in faithful user simulation and evaluation, connecting algorithmic advances with real-world applications.

I have worked on industrial recommendation at Taobao through the RecGPT series, and on reasoning for industrial ranking through OnePiece. My recent interests include adaptive computation, recommendation scaling, and agents that learn from experience.
